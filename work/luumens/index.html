Luumens

Luumens supplies photovoltaic panels, Huawei inverters, energy storage and methanol and hydrogen fuel cells for land and marine use. The site is for customers looking to buy, and it also has to explain the benefits of the products, and of alternative energy itself.

    The
    brief

    Products, and the case for alternative energy.
    Design

    From scratch.

    The page flow, layouts and visual design. The company set the goal and the information that mattered; everything else was mine.

    Development

    Custom widgets only.

    Every section is a custom HTML widget in Elementor, none of its drag-and-drop widgets, with hand-written CSS and GSAP motion.

    Starting point

    More weight, more motion.

    The earlier site had little motion, and its layouts didn't give the important sections and headings the weight they needed.

    The brief

    A site for customers looking to buy, that also explains the benefits of the products, and of alternative energy itself.

    Three
    decisions

    How the pages are ordered.
    01 · Story first

    The energy, before the products.

    The homepage opens on an illustrated energy cycle, how clean power is made and used, ahead of anything for sale.

    02 · By situation

    Start from where you need power.

    Six photographic cards (ship, camping, RV, signal tower, emergency rescue and monitoring) each lead to a page for that setting.

    03 · Figures up front

    Products lead with the numbers.

    Product pages open on the product and its key figures as a bento grid of cards, then explain the technology card by card. The bento product page is the part I'm proudest of.

    Up
    close

    Details from the build.

    How it's
    evolved

    The homepage, reworked.

    The homepage changed the most. My first layout didn't flow well, so I reworked it for a clearer path through the site and easier navigation. The latest update opens up the hero, lets products lead with their figures and gives every page more room.

    The hard
    part

    Every screen size.

    Working out a layout that properly represents the company, and making every hand-coded section work at every size. Each layout was designed and tested for widescreen, laptop, tablet and phone: on phones the solution cards become a swipeable row, product figures restack and the menu opens into a full-screen list.

    Next
    time

    Expectations first.

    I'd pin down everyone's expectations and preferences carefully at the start, so there are fewer rounds of changes later.

    Outcome

    Launched in 2026

    A reworked homepage, product pages that lead with the numbers, motion throughout and layouts tuned for every screen size. Designed, built and still refined by me.
