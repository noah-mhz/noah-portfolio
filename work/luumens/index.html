Brief

Clean energy, explained before it's sold.

Luumens supplies solar, energy storage and methanol and hydrogen fuel cells for land and marine use. Most of it is new to visitors, so the site has to explain the technology, meet people in their own situation and make the specs easy to scan.

Design
The page flow, layouts and visual design, from scratch. The client set the goal and the information that mattered; everything else was mine.
Development
Every section is a custom HTML widget in Elementor — none of its drag-and-drop widgets — with hand-written CSS and GSAP motion.
Delivery
Built and tested for widescreen, laptop, tablet and phone. Launched in 2026, and I keep refining it.
Collaboration
Solo, working directly with the client.
Three decisions

Explain first, sell second.

  1. Story first

    The energy, before the products.

    The homepage opens on an illustrated energy cycle — how clean power is made and used — ahead of anything for sale.

  2. By situation

    Start from where you need power.

    Six photographic cards — ship, camping, RV, signal tower, emergency rescue and monitoring — each lead to a page for that setting.

  3. Figures up front

    Products lead with the numbers.

    Product pages open on the product and the figures buyers ask for, then explain the technology card by card.

How it's evolved

Built, thenrefined.

I built the first version of the site and keep improving it. The latest update opens up the hero, lets products lead with their figures and gives every page more room.

Design system

One red, one card system.

  • Luumens red#E2323F
  • Ink#1C1C1C
  • Mist#F7F7F7
  • White#FFFFFF
The hard part — responsive

Hand-coded,at every size.

With every section written by hand rather than built from Elementor's widgets, each layout had to be designed and tested for widescreen, laptop, tablet and phone. On phones the solution cards become a swipeable row, product figures restack and the menu opens into a full-screen list.

OutcomeLaunched in 2026 — designed, built and still refined by me.
