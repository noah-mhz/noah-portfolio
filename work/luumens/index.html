Context

Clean energy, explained before it's sold.

Luumens is a Singapore alternative energy solutions provider built on the generation of clean energy. It designs and installs commercial and residential solar — including BIPV and BAPV such as glass canopies, solar tiles and facades — builds energy storage and EV charger installations, and supplies methanol and hydrogen fuel cells for land and marine use. The site had to make that range, and the technology behind it, easy to follow.

Approach

Show the system, then the products.

Fuel cells and methanol are unfamiliar to most visitors, so the homepage opens on how the energy is made before it shows anything for sale. From there, every solution and product page answers one question — where does this fit, and why is it better — in a clean, technical layout led by the Luumens red.

  • Technology first

    The energy-cycle diagram leads the homepage, ahead of the products.

  • A story per use

    Each solution page starts from the limit people hit in that setting, and how a fuel cell answers it.

  • Specs you can scan

    Product details become cards, figures and a decay chart rather than a datasheet.

Solutions

Six settings, six stories.

Ship, camping, RV, signal tower, emergency rescue and monitoring — each gets its own photograph on the homepage, its own card in the solutions carousel and its own page, written around the people who would use it there.

RV energy

Power thatgoes off‑grid.

The page leads with a real proof point — a methanol-powered off-road RV shown at the 18th Shanghai International RV Exhibition, delivering over 30kWh of continuous power from 30L of methanol — then sets out four benefits and hands on to the next solution.

Product — PV panel

A datasheet, made scannable.

The N-type bifacial PV panel page sets each advantage in its own card — bifaciality, attenuation, efficiency, temperature co-efficient — next to the panel itself, then gives the warranties as figures and the power decay as a chart.
