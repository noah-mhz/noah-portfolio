Context

Heavy engineering, presented with calm.

Voyagaer engineers water, wastewater and fuel systems for marine and industrial applications — sewage treatment plants, oily water separators, freshwater makers, hydrophore systems and more — and supports them across their whole lifecycle. The site had to speak to engineers and procurement teams at once: precise enough to specify from, calm enough to trust.

My role
Design system and build — a shared design-token system for type, colour and spacing, every section a custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript widget in WordPress (Elementor), and GSAP/ScrollTrigger motion.
The hard part
Many independently built widgets living on the same pages. I scoped each widget's JavaScript so sections never conflict, aligned their tokens so everything reads as one site, and built a filterable catalogue of nine systems with full technical spec sheets.

Approach

Automotive calm, engineering depth.

The design language borrows the restraint of a premium automotive brand — thin lowercase headings, generous space, one deep sea teal — and pairs it with the depth engineers expect: line drawings instead of renders, full specification sheets and clear routes to an enquiry. Motion is slow and deliberate: clip-path wipes on entry, scroll-triggered cards and quiet parallax.

  • Drawings, not renders

    Every product is shown as a technical line drawing — the language of the people who specify it.

  • Depth on demand

    Product pages open simply, then go as deep as needed: process, compliance and the full tech sheet.

  • Slow, deliberate motion

    GSAP clip-path wipes and paced scroll reveals, with fast, gimmicky hover effects removed.

Product catalog

Nine systems,one catalog.

The full range in one place: search, filter by category, application or system type, and see each product's status at a glance before going deeper.

Product pages

Read like engineering.

Each product opens on deep teal with its own drawing and a one-line promise — for the hydrophore system, constant pressurised freshwater wherever it's needed — then tabs through process and features, compliance and the technical specifications.

Industries

At sea, in plants, in cities.

Industries are grouped by where the systems run — marine, industrial and civil — each with its own photography, a short description and the systems that apply, shown as tags.

Identity

Deep sea teal, thin type.

  • Deep sea#002E3B
  • Voyagaer teal#48BDA9
  • Mist#F4F7F7
  • White#FFFFFF

Artifakt Thin sets the lowercase display headings, Artifakt Regular the second tier, and Aptos the body. The radiating teal mark is the one flourish; deep sea footers map the whole site.

Around the site

Built to be looked after.

Beyond the products, the site sells what happens after installation — commissioning, training, spare parts, maintenance, surveys and retrofits — and the company's commitment to long-term, responsible partnerships.
