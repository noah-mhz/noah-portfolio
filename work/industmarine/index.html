Industmarine

The website for Industmarine Engineers, a Singapore supplier of marine, offshore and industrial equipment with three decades in the trade. Eight pages, all laid out like an engineering drawing sheet.

    The idea

    Built like the equipment it sells: every page is laid out like an engineering drawing sheet.

    Three
    decisions

    Built like the equipment it sells.
    01 · The drawing sheet

    Every page is a sheet.

    A flexible rail, four content columns and another rail. Every content cell is outlined, and ruler ticks run down both outer edges.

    02 · Drawings, not icons

    Line drawings with dimensions.

    Sectors, services and contact channels are shown as technical elevations with dimension lines, which plot themselves as they scroll in.

    03 · One catalogue

    Twenty products, one page.

    Products filter by competency or principal and open in a details drawer, so seven principals' equipment stays in one place.

    Up
    close

    Details from the build.

    The proudest
    part

    The engineering-sheet grid.

    The grid is drawn, not decorated. Lines come from each cell's own edges and are de-duplicated, so they never cross content, and matching elements on the same row share one height. The same sheet carries on into the footer, in light lines on navy.

    The hard
    part

    Responsiveness.

    A drawing sheet has to stay a sheet at every size. On wide screens the content area widens in steps; in narrow four-column cells the titles scale so long words never overflow; on tablets and phones the grid reflows without losing its outlines.

    Next
    time

    A lighter build.

    Fewer page-level overrides, and a shared widget library the pages draw from.

    Outcome

    Live, 2026

    Live at industmarine.com. Eight pages: home, about, competencies, products, partners, industries, services and contact.
