About

I like building things that shouldn't feel like templates.

I'm Noah (Zaw Min Htut), an independent web designer and developer in Singapore.

I handle the whole job — layout, motion and the code that ships — so what you approve is what goes live.

In-house, I design and build the websites for Pomar Engineering's marine and energy brands; on my own, I take freelance projects like Ganko. Before that I spent close to three years at Thales DIS as a software developer, automating card validation and testing releases, after internships in full-stack web and industrial IoT. That engineering background is why the details matter to me: sites that hold up on every screen and stay easy for your team to update.
