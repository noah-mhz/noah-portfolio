About

I like building things that shouldn't feel like templates.

I'm Noah (Zaw Min Htut) — a creative developer and web designer working in the space between design and engineering.

Websites, interfaces and interactive products — designed with editorial restraint and engineered to be fast, accessible and responsive on every screen.

Today I design and build websites for marine, energy and hospitality brands. Before that I spent close to three years at Thales DIS as a software developer — automating card validation and testing releases — after internships in full-stack web and industrial IoT. That engineering background is why the details matter: the things I make are built to hold up, not just to impress on first load.
