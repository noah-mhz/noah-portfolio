Case study · Freelance · Restaurant · 2025

Ganko.sg

The restaurant's first website. Ganko Sushi began in 1963 as a small eatery in Osaka. Its Singapore restaurant had no website before this one, so I designed it from scratch.

Client
Ganko Sushi Singapore · Freelance
Role
Web design & development
Platform
GoDaddy Website Builder
Live site

screens

    Three
    decisions

    History first, booking last.
    01 · Heritage first

    The story starts in Osaka.

    The About pages set archival photographs of the founding team beside today's Singapore kitchen, on a washi-paper background.

    02 · Photography leads

    The food and the room do the talking.

    Copy stays short. Dishes, interiors and the chefs at work carry each page, set in an asymmetric gallery of varied crops and heights.

    03 · Booking on-site

    Book a table without leaving.

    The Chope booking widget is embedded in the Reservation page, beside the address and opening hours, so guests never leave ganko.sg to book.

    The hard
    part

    Working around the builder.

    GoDaddy's builder was the client's choice: their team already knew it, so they can keep the site up to date themselves. But it's very limited in functionality, layouts and fonts. The layouts I first built from its blocks didn't hold up, so I rebuilt those sections by hand as custom HTML and kept the builder's own layouts only where they worked.

    Outcome

    Launched in 2025

    The restaurant's first website. Eight pages with the menu, the story from Osaka and booking through an embedded Chope widget, on a builder the restaurant's team can update themselves.
