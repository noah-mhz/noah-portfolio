How this site was made: one stretchable typeface, a three-colour palette that flips between light and dark, and a handful of moving parts built by hand.

Type

Archivo does almost everything. It's a variable font with a width axis, so the same letters run condensed for section titles, wide for the hero and plain for reading. Geist Mono handles the small print: labels, dates and counters.

Colour

Both themes share three colours on a 60 / 30 / 10 split. Dark is light turned inside out: the ground and the ink swap places, and apricot stays the accent.

Apricot is too faint on peach for small text, so in the light theme links, labels and thin lines use burnt apricot instead. Switching theme draws blinds in the next theme's ground, swaps the colours behind them, then lifts them.
