Type

Two typefaces,two jobs.

Inter Tight carries the voice — huge and tight for headlines, quiet for reading. JetBrains Mono handles the data: labels, coordinates, dates and the clock. Both are self-hosted, and only the weights the site uses are loaded.

Colour

Near-black, off-white,one mint.

  • Ink#0A0A0A
  • Paper#F2F2F0
  • Muted#858585
  • Mint#A6FFCB

Mint is the only colour, and it's used sparingly: links, the form, progress lines and one word per section. On each case study it steps aside for the client's own colour.

  • Ganko.sg
  • Luumens
  • Voyagaer
Grid & viewfinder

Four columns anda viewfinder.

The four-column grid is never hidden — it draws itself in when the site opens and brightens at the contact. Corner marks borrowed from a camera viewfinder frame whatever you're looking at: project images, buttons, and the cursor itself.

Point at it
Motion

Slow, deliberate,never in the way.

  • Easing

    Expo and power-four curves for entrances and wipes. Nothing bounces.

  • Timing

    About 0.9 seconds for everyday reveals; up to 2 seconds for the opening moments.

  • Three versions

    Every effect has a desktop version, a touch version and a still one for reduced motion.

  • Cheap to run

    Only transform, opacity and clip-path move. Videos load late and play only while on screen.
