Welcome to the Other side

The professional side is next door. This side is where I play: four experiments in variable type, motion and interface, built by hand with no templates.

01

Stretch

One typeface, two axes. Grab the word and pull: sideways stretches the letters you hold, up and down changes their weight. Let go and it springs back.

Stretch
02

Pressure

A field of letters that remembers where you've been. Move across it to leave a trail; click or tap to send a ripple through it.

03

Helix

Every screen from the work and the concepts, wound into a helix that screws along its own axis. Drag to spin it, or unwind it into a wall. Open the one in front.

04

Concepts pile

Homepages I designed and built to pitch small businesses, printed and dropped on the table. Throw them around; double-click one, or press Enter, to open it.
