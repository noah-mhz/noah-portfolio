# Crumb & Kin — homepage concept (anonymised)

A neutral copy of a small-batch bakery homepage Noah designed as a pitch. The brand, story,
address and copy are placeholders; photos are stock (Pexels).

Single `index.html` with inline CSS/JS; GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger from cdnjs. Fonts are bundled
in `assets/fonts`. Bakes, signature list and gallery live in data arrays at the top of the script.
