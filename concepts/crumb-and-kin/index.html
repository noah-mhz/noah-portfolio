Our story

From our home to yours.

Crumb & Kin is a mother-and-daughter bakery. It started at a home kitchen table, grew through weekend pop-ups, and now has a small shopfront of its own.

Two generations, one kitchen, and a shared love for making good things.

kin /kɪn/noun — your family; the people you share a table with.
