About these

Pitched, not commissioned.

Homepages I designed and built to pitch small businesses in Singapore: a trading card shop and a rösti café. To show them here, each business's name, logo, photos, address and social handles are replaced with placeholders and original illustrations. The layouts, motion and code are mine.

01 / 02 — Card shop

Pixel Pull, pitched for Shopify.

A trading card store's homepage, proposed as a future Shopify Online Store 2.0 build. The pitch carried a design note on every section, so the owner could read the reasoning as they scrolled.

Type
Anton for display, Inter for body, Silkscreen for pixel labels.
Platform
Static prototype, planned as a Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme.
Built
Nav, product grid with filters, bag drawer and poster lightbox, running on sample product data.
Hand-over
Every section maps to a Shopify section the owner edits without code (below).
Pixel Pull — design notes

The why, section by section.

  1. Why

    Hero

    Black stage + orange pops = instant brand recognition. Cards float to say “TCG store” in one glance.

  2. Why

    Categories

    Asymmetric tiles, each its own colour and size. Big for the best sellers, small for the rest. Not a Shopify grid.

  3. Why

    Products

    Clean, identical cards so the product is the star. Filter by TCG now; set, language and stock next. Try Add to bag.

  4. Why

    Campaigns

    Posters stay in their original loud colours, framed by a calm layout. Owner uploads new art in Shopify, no developer needed.

  5. Why

    Events

    Tournaments and lucky draws are their edge. Bold layout turns the store into a community hub.

  6. Why

    About

    Short and human. Logo + real shop photo build trust. No invented backstory.

  7. Why

    Store

    Orange block breaks the page rhythm and drives walk-ins. Details are editable fields.

  8. Why

    Social

    Keeps the Instagram energy in a tidy grid. One tap to follow.

02 / 02 — Café

Little Rösti, content in one file.

A rösti café's homepage. Every word, price and image lives in one content file, so the owner's updates never touch the layout, and the content can later move to Shopify metafields or a CMS.

Content
Text, menu, prices, hours and images, all in one file.
Built from data
The menu, best sellers, story and gallery are built from that file.
Announcements
One promo slot for holiday hours, new menu drops and events; swap the poster to match.
Motion
GSAP and ScrollTrigger, with a reduced-motion path.
